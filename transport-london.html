Transport — Getting Around London

Bus & Tube: The Oyster Card Life

You can travel on London buses and the Underground using:

  • Oyster card (reloadable travel card)
  • Contactless debit/credit card (often cheaper thanks to daily caps)
Pro Tip: Most people just use their contactless card these days. It's simpler and applies the same daily/weekly caps as Oyster automatically.

Student & Railcard Magic ✨

Link your 16–25 Railcard, 26–30 Railcard, or Student Oyster to your account and get ⅓ off off-peak fares.

Absolute game-changer. (Yes, off-peak times are explained below—no panic.)

What Is Off-Peak Time?

Off-peak = cheaper fares + fewer crowds.

Monday–Friday

  • Before 6:30am
  • 9:30am–4pm
  • After 7pm

Weekends & Bank Holidays

All day 🎉

Avoid rush hour (6:30–9:30am and 4–7pm) and you'll save money and sanity.

Quick Tips for London Transport

  • Always stand on the right on escalators (walk on the left)
  • Let people off the train before boarding
  • Check for weekend closures on TfL website
  • Night Tube runs Friday & Saturday nights on some lines
  • Buses are cashless—card or Oyster only

Welcome to London transport. It's actually pretty good once you get the hang of it! 🚇
