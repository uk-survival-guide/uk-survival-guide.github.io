Coaches Between Cities

Coaches Companies 🚌

  • National Express – Reliable, student discounts available
  • FlixBus – Budget European service, often £5–£10 deals
  • Megabus – Ultra-budget (£1 tickets do exist)
Top Tip: Coaches take longer but are unbeatable on price. Bring snacks, download Netflix, and enjoy the countryside.

UK transport outside London takes a bit of getting used to, but once you know the systems, it's pretty manageable. Safe travels! 🚆
