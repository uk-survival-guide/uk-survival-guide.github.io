Water

Who Supplies Your Water?

Your water supplier depends entirely on your postcode — you usually can’t choose your provider.

Major UK Water Companies

  • Thames Water – London & Thames Valley
  • Southern Water – South East
  • Anglian Water – East England
  • United Utilities – North West
  • Yorkshire Water – Yorkshire
  • Severn Trent – Midlands
  • South West Water – Devon & Cornwall
  • Welsh Water – Wales
  • Scottish Water – Scotland (often included in council tax)

How You’re Charged

  • Fixed charge – Same amount every year
  • Metered – Pay only for what you use
Top Tip:

Living alone? A water meter can often save you money.
