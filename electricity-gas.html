Electricity & Gas ⚡️

A small habit goes a long way — double-check all the lights are off when you’re not home. Save energy, save money, and a few trees along the way.

Keeping the Lights On

Unlike water, you can choose your electricity and gas supplier — and switching is often the smartest move.

Main Suppliers

  • Octopus
  • British Gas
  • EDF
  • E.ON Next
  • OVO
  • Shell Energy
  • Scottish Power
  • Bulb (now part of Octopus)

Tariffs Explained

  • Standard Variable – Flexible but usually pricey
  • Fixed – Stable prices for 1–2 years
  • Economy 7 / 10 – Cheaper electricity overnight
Top Tip:

Use comparison sites like Uswitch or MoneySuperMarket. Loyalty does not pay.

Communal Heating (Hot Water Bills)

Common in new builds and high-rise flats, communal heating systems supply hot water and heating to the entire building.

Common Providers

  • Switch2
  • E.ON
  • Vattenfall
  • SSE Energy Solutions
  • Pinnacle Power
  • Heat Trust (regulator)

Charges Usually Include

  • Standing charge
  • Usage charge
Top Tip:

These bills can be shockingly high — monitor your meters and always question estimated charges.
