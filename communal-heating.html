Communal Heating 🏢🔥

If you live in a high-rise or modern apartment block, your heating and hot water may come from a communal system rather than a boiler inside your flat.

What Is Communal Heating?

A central energy centre supplies hot water and heating to the entire building. Your flat receives heat through pipes — you don’t control the main boiler.

Common in

  • New-build apartments
  • High-rise residential blocks
  • Build-to-rent developments
  • Regeneration estates

Who Bills You?

You usually cannot choose your communal heating provider. They are appointed by the building owner or managing agent.

Common Providers (UK / London)

  • E.ON Heat
  • Evinox Residential
  • Metergy
  • Insite Energy
  • Vital Energi

How Charges Work

Typical Bill Structure

  • Standing charge – daily fixed cost
  • Usage charge – based on heat or hot water used
  • Maintenance fees – sometimes hidden inside tariffs
Reality Check:

Communal heating is often marketed as “efficient”, but bills can be surprisingly high, especially with poor insulation or estimated readings.

What You Can Do

  • Submit meter readings regularly
  • Check whether bills are estimated
  • Request a tariff breakdown
  • Raise issues with the managing agent if costs spike
Top Tip:

If something feels wrong, question it early. Communal heat billing errors are common — and rarely fixed automatically.
