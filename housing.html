House Hunting

Finding Your Cosy Flat

Whether you're after a room, a whole flat, or something in between, these are the main platforms people actually use:

🛏 Just need a room?

SpareRoom — The go-to for finding single rooms in shared houses. Great filters, realistic prices, and very London.

🏠 Looking for a whole flat?

  • Rightmove – The biggest property portal in the UK
  • Zoopla – Excellent for comparisons and area insights
  • OpenRent – Rent directly from landlords (no agent fees!)

Estate Agents: The Main Players

Most listings on the big websites will connect you to one of these agents:

Foxtons · Martyn Gerrard · KFH · Winkworth · Dexters · Hamptons · Savills · Knight Frank · Chestertons · Portico · Marsh & Parsons · Benham & Reeves · Northwood · Your Move · Leaders

Top Tip: Estate agents can be lovely—but always:
  • Read reviews
  • Check break clauses
  • Confirm what fees (if any) apply
  • Never rush a contract because "someone else is interested"

What to Look For When Viewing

  • Water pressure — Turn on the shower and taps
  • Mould and damp — Check corners, windows, and behind furniture
  • Heating — Ask about the boiler age and how heating is controlled
  • Storage — UK flats are notoriously small
  • Transport links — Check how long your commute actually takes
  • Noise — Visit at different times if possible

Questions to Ask

  • What bills are included?
  • When is the property available?
  • How long is the contract?
  • Is there a break clause?
  • What's the deposit amount?
  • Are there any known issues with the property?
⚠️ Red Flags:
  • Pressure to pay a deposit immediately
  • Requests to pay before viewing
  • Landlords who won't provide references
  • Prices that seem too good to be true
  • No proper tenancy agreement

The Rental Process

  1. Viewing — Book and attend property viewings
  2. Application — Submit references, proof of income, ID
  3. Right to Rent check — Landlords must verify your legal right to rent in the UK
  4. Contract — Read everything carefully before signing
  5. Deposit — Usually 4-6 weeks rent, must be protected in a government scheme
  6. Move in — Take photos of everything and note any existing damage
Money-Saving Tip: Consider sharing with others. A room in a shared house is often half the price of a studio flat, and you'll meet people too.
