About This Booklet

Moving to the UK is exciting… until you realise you suddenly need to understand:

  • Housing
  • Transport
  • Bills
  • How to survive adult life without Googling everything at 2am

The goal of this booklet is simple: remove the admin chaos so you can actually enjoy living here.

Think of it as a friendly cuppa and a chat about UK adulting — minus the stress, plus a bit of British charm ☕️

House Hunting

Finding Your Castle (or Cosy Flat)

Whether you're after a room, a whole flat, or something in between:

  • SpareRoom — single rooms in shared houses
  • Rightmove — biggest UK portal
  • Zoopla — great comparisons
  • OpenRent — rent direct from landlords

Estate agents can be lovely — but always read reviews, check break clauses, confirm fees, and never rush because "someone else is interested".

Transport — Getting Around London

  • Oyster card
  • Contactless debit / credit card (daily caps)

Link a Railcard or Student Oyster and get ⅓ off off-peak fares. Absolute game-changer.

I've only seen a rat on the Tube twice. Relax.

Off-peak = cheaper, quieter, happier travel.

Transport — UK Cities

  • Manchester - Bee Network
  • Nottingham — NET
  • Sheffield — Supertram
  • Edinburgh — Trams

Outside London, many buses still accept cash. Each city has its own rules — download the local app.

Trains Between Cities

Trainline is convenient but charges a small fee. If you travel often, book direct.

Train delay refunds are real. Claim them.

Coaches

  • National Express
  • FlixBus
  • Megabus

Slower than trains — unbeatable on price. Bring snacks and download Netflix.

Water Bills

Your supplier depends on postcode.

Living alone? A meter often saves money.

Council Tax

Charged per property, not per person.

  • Students — £0
  • Single occupant — 25% off
Never ignore council tax letters. They escalate fast.

Electricity & Gas

Octopus, EDF, British Gas, OVO are common.

Loyalty does not pay. Switch regularly.

Communal Heating

Common in new builds and high-rise flats.

Bills can be shockingly high — monitor meters.

NHS & National Insurance Numbers

Register with a GP before you're sick.

These two numbers quietly control adult life.

5 Must-NOT-Dos

  • Ignore council tax
  • Assume housemates sorted bills
  • Pay bills not in your name
  • Don't ignore final notices
  • Don't assume housemates sorted everything

5 Must-DOs

  • Photograph meters
  • Track payments
  • Register early
  • Keep a "Boring Adult Stuff" folder
  • Join local Facebook & community groups

Final Thoughts

You're not bad at this — it's genuinely confusing. Everyone struggles at first (yes, even British people). Most problems can be solved with couple of calls and a bit of patience.

Make a cuppa. You've got this ☕️🇬🇧
