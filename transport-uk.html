Transport — Getting Around the UK

Trams & City Transport Systems

Many UK cities have their own integrated transport networks:

  • Manchester → Bee Network (trams + buses on one system)
  • Nottingham → Nottingham Express Transit (NET)
  • Sheffield → Supertram
  • Edinburgh → Edinburgh Trams
  • Birmingham → West Midlands Metro
  • Croydon / South London → London Trams

Buses Outside London

Unlike London, many UK cities still allow cash payment on buses.

This is common in: Manchester · Birmingham · Liverpool · Leeds · Bristol …and most smaller towns

Have coins ready and ask for a single or day ticket.

Top Tip: Each city sets its own fares and rules—download the local transport app when you arrive.

Trains Between Cities 🚆

The Trainline App

One app for almost all UK trains:

  • Small booking fee (~£0.75)
  • Excellent UI
  • Digital tickets
  • Price comparisons across operators

Booking Direct (No Booking Fee!)

If you travel the same route often, book directly:

  • Avanti West Coast – London ↔ Manchester, Liverpool, Glasgow
  • LNER – London ↔ York, Newcastle, Scotland (East Coast)
  • GWR – London ↔ Bristol, Bath, Cardiff, Cornwall
  • CrossCountry – Long-distance routes across the UK
  • Northern – Regional services in the North
  • ScotRail – Scotland
  • Southeastern – London ↔ Kent & East Sussex
  • South Western Railway – London ↔ Southampton, Portsmouth

Train Delay Refunds (Yes, This Is Real)

If your train is late:

  • 15–29 mins → 25% refund
  • 30–59 mins → 50% refund
  • 60+ mins / cancelled → 100% refund
How to Claim: Claim via the train company or Trainline. Refunds usually arrive within 5–10 days.

UK transport outside London takes a bit of getting used to, but once you know the systems, it's pretty manageable. Safe travels! 🚆
