About This Booklet

Welcome to Your UK Survival Guide 🇬🇧

Moving to the UK is exciting… until you realise you suddenly need to understand:

  • Housing
  • Bills
  • Transport
  • And how to survive adult life without Googling everything at 2am

The goal of this booklet is simple: remove the admin chaos so you can actually enjoy living here.

Bills have a knack for turning up everywhere—like pigeons in Trafalgar Square. This pocket guide gathers the most common suppliers, real-life tips, and things people only learn after messing it up once.

Think of it as a friendly cuppa and a chat about UK adulting— minus the stress, plus a bit of British charm ☕️

Why This Guide Exists

Every year, thousands of people move to the UK for work, study, or a fresh start. And every year, they discover the same frustrating truth: nobody tells you about the boring stuff until it's already a problem.

You can research the best neighborhoods, find the perfect flat, and plan your entire life—but then you get hit with:

  • "Wait, what's council tax?"
  • "Why are there so many energy suppliers?"
  • "How do I register with the NHS?"
  • "Why is my water bill so high?"
Top Tip: This guide won't solve every problem, but it will give you a head start on the ones that catch most people off guard.

How to Use This Guide

You don't need to read this cover to cover. Jump to whatever section you need right now:

  • Just arrived? Start with House Hunting and Transport
  • Setting up bills? Check Water, Energy, and Council Tax
  • Need healthcare? Go straight to NHS Registration
  • Feeling overwhelmed? Read the 5 Must-DOs and Must-NOT-Dos

A Quick Reality Check

The UK is wonderful. It's also bureaucratic, quirky, and sometimes baffling. Things that seem simple—like paying a bill or booking a doctor's appointment—can have unexpected twists.

But here's the good news: once you understand the system, it actually works pretty well. And you're already ahead by reading this.

Remember: Everyone struggles with this at first. Even British people find it confusing. You're doing great just by being here and preparing yourself.

What This Guide Covers

  • Housing: Where to look, what to watch out for
  • Transport: Getting around London and the rest of the UK
  • Bills: Water, energy, council tax, and communal heating
  • Healthcare: NHS registration and National Insurance
  • Survival Tips: The things nobody tells you until it's too late
